# 3. Risk Evaluation

**Learning Objective:** Evaluate treatment options using cost-benefit analysis

---

### Scenario

**SecureBank** has a Critical risk with ALE = $300,000

**Proposed Mitigation:**

- Implementation cost: $80,000
- Annual maintenance: $15,000
- Expected risk reduction: 75%

---

### Exercise

**Complete cost-benefit analysis:**

| Metric | Calculation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Current ALE | Given | $300,000 |
| New ALE after mitigation | $300,000 × (1 - 0.75) = $300,000 × 0.25 | **$75,000** |
| Annual Savings | Current ALE - New ALE = $300,000 - $75,000 | **$225,000** |
| 3-Year Total Cost | $80,000 + ($15,000 × 3) = $80,000 + $45,000 | **$125,000** |
| 3-Year Total Savings | Annual Savings × 3 = $225,000 × 3 | **$675,000** |
| Net Benefit | Savings - Cost = $675,000 - $125,000 | **$550,000** |
| ROI | (Net Benefit / Cost) × 100 = ($550,000 / $125,000) × 100 | **440%** |

**Decision:**

- [x] Implement mitigation (cost-effective)
- [ ] Reject mitigation (not cost-effective)

**Justification:** The mitigation costs **$125,000** over 3 years but saves **$675,000** in expected losses, giving a net benefit of **$550,000** and an ROI of **440%**. In simple words, every $1 spent brings back $4.40 in avoided losses on top of the original $1. The yearly savings ($225,000) are much higher than the yearly cost, so the control pays for itself in the first year. Since this is also a **Critical** risk, reducing it by 75% protects the bank and its customers, so the mitigation should be implemented.
